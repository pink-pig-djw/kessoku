1. id "bg_aquarium": TIME OF YEAR: January (winter holiday). Inside the aquarium at Hakkeijima Sea Paradise near Kanazawa-hakkei, Yokohama: a long dim walkway tunnel running under a huge blue water tank, the curved acrylic glass overhead and on both sides, schools of small silver fish and a ray gliding past, wavering blue light patterns on the floor and on the handrail. Calm, dark, quiet and safe like a deep blue cave. No people.
2. id "bg_nijika_room": TIME OF YEAR: February. Ijichi Nijika's small bedroom in the sisters' home on the third floor of the old building above the live house STARRY in Shimokitazawa: a neat single bed with a yellow blanket, a drum practice pad and a pair of drumsticks on the desk, a small mirror, polka-dot cushions and a polka-dot hair ribbon on a hook, a few band flyers and a calendar full of handwritten schedules on the wall, a window with winter afternoon light. Tidy, warm and homely.
3. id "bg_kita_home": TIME OF YEAR: March, early spring. The living room of an ordinary, very tidy Japanese middle-class home of two civil servant parents in the Tokyo suburbs: a low table with tea cups on a rug, a sofa, a TV cabinet, a tall bookshelf packed with literature novels and old hardcovers, a few framed photos of a red-haired girl growing up, soft afternoon light through lace curtains.
4. id "bg_ochanomizu": TIME OF YEAR: January, clear cold winter day. The famous musical instrument street in Ochanomizu, Tokyo (the Meidai-dori slope): a row of big guitar and drum shops with walls of guitars visible through the glass windows, colorful banners and shop signs (all unreadable), a gentle downhill slope, bare street trees, crisp winter sunlight.
