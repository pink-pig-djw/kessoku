Ijichi Nijika's small bedroom in the sisters' home on the third floor of the old building above the live house STARRY in Shimokitazawa: a neat single bed with a yellow blanket, a drum practice pad and a pair of drumsticks on the desk, a small mirror, polka-dot cushions and a polka-dot hair ribbon on a hook, a few band flyers and a calendar full of handwritten schedules on the wall, a window with winter afternoon light. Tidy, warm and homely.
